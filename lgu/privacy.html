Privacy notice

How the Municipality of Calatrava handles personal information on this website, issued under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

Last reviewed: 10 September 2026

What this website collects

This website does not collect personal information from visitors. It is a set of static pages. There are no accounts, no login, no online forms, no payment processing, and no advertising or analytics trackers. The municipality does not place cookies on your device from this site, and does not build profiles of visitors.

If and when online transactions are added — appointment booking, document requests, or a feedback form — this notice will be updated first to state exactly what is collected, why, how long it is kept, and who may see it.

Services from other organisations

Some pages load content from other organisations. When your browser loads that content, those organisations may receive technical information such as your IP address and the page you are viewing, under their own privacy policies rather than ours:

  • Google Fonts — supplies the typefaces used across the site.
  • Google Maps — the location map embedded on the Contact page. This one may set cookies.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website and keeps standard server logs of requests.

Links to the municipality's Facebook page, and to any other external site, take you outside this website. We do not control what those sites collect.

When you contact us

If you email or call an office listed in our directory, that office receives whatever information you choose to send. It is used only to respond to your request and to carry out the office's official functions, and is handled under the Data Privacy Act and the municipality's records retention rules.

Information published on this site

This website publishes the names, positions, and official contact details of elected officials and department heads, and documents required by the DILG Full Disclosure Policy. These are published because law and regulation require public disclosure of them, not on the basis of consent.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to object, to access, to rectify, to erase or block, to damages, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint. To exercise any of these, contact the Data Protection Officer below. You may also complain directly to the National Privacy Commission (opens in a new tab).

Data Protection Officer

The Municipality of Calatrava is in the process of formally designating its Data Protection Officer. Until that designation is published here, privacy questions and requests may be addressed to the Office of the Municipal Mayor, whose contact details are on the Contact page.

Changes to this notice

This notice will be revised whenever the website changes how it handles personal information. The review date at the top shows when it was last updated.
